Standalone CPU WAV-to-RTTM CLI, derived from the June diarizer at `b0424b695` (2026-06-11), removed from the product in `805084073`; source provenance is that history and the seeded read-only refs. Carries local copies of diarizer, clustering, segmenter and metrics, with all executable thresholds/defaults unchanged; threshold and gate explanations are retained verbatim from the seeded June sources.
`run.sh` resolves the repo from its own location, sets NODE_PATH to the seeded mixed-pipeline node_modules, and runs its tsx with the local tsconfig. Root tsc checks the same dependency through baseUrl/paths; this is dependency resolution only, with no imports from product source and no package/lockfile changes.
`main.ts` reads PCM16 WAV, feeds 20 ms Float32 chunks with audio-time timestamps, records committed turns, flushes buffered speech at EOF, applies commit/cluster rewrites and clips times to the WAV. Adaptations explicitly select CPU and propagate inference failures to exit 2 and exit 0 after the RTTM write completes; no model or clustering tuning. The single committed speaker per turn is preserved, including on overlap segments; no extra overlap speaker is invented.
`run.sh --help` requires no model; actual WAV runs download WeSpeaker ResNet34-LM and pyannote segmentation through transformers.js into the system temporary directory's `diarization-eval-models` cache. All model-loading execution and real-model DER are caller-verified outside the sandbox.
