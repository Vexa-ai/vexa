---
title: Meeting series / community
description: A recurring meeting or community — members, notes for every session, a decisions log.
when: A weekly team sync, a reading group, a community call or any meeting that repeats with the same people.
---
## Creates
- README.md — what this workspace is, one paragraph
- overview.md — purpose, cadence, where it happens, how to join
- members.md — who takes part and their role
- notes.md — one section per session, newest first
- decisions.md — decisions across all sessions
- agenda-next.md — the running agenda for the next session

## Questions
- `series_name` — What is the series called? (default: Our meeting)
- `cadence` — How often does it meet (e.g. every Tuesday 17:00)?
- `purpose` — What is it for, in one sentence?

## Suggested routines
- **Agenda reminder** — a day before each session, post agenda-next.md to the members.
- **Session notes** — after each session, add the transcript's summary, decisions and actions to notes.md and decisions.md, then reset agenda-next.md.
