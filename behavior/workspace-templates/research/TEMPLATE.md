## Creates
- README.md — what this workspace is, one paragraph
- question.md — the question, why it matters, what would answer it
- sources.md — every source with what it says and how much to trust it
- interviews.md — notes from each conversation
- findings.md — what the evidence says, claim by claim
- answer.md — the conclusion and its confidence

## Questions
- `question` — What question are you trying to answer?
- `decision` — What decision will the answer inform?
- `due` — When do you need an answer?

## Suggested routines
- **Interview notes** — after each recorded interview, add its notes to interviews.md and any new claims to findings.md with the transcript as the source.
