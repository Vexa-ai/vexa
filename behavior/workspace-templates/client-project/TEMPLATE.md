title: Client project
description: Run work for a client — scope, contacts, meetings, decisions, deliverables.
when: Delivering a project or engagement for an outside client with a scope and a deadline.
---
## Creates
- README.md — what this workspace is, one paragraph
- overview.md — client, goal, scope, timeline, contacts
- meetings.md — every client meeting, its notes and actions
- decisions.md — what was agreed, when, by whom
- deliverables.md — what we owe and when, with status
- risks.md — what could go wrong and what we do about it

## Questions
- `client` — Who is the client?
- `project` — What is the project called? (default: Client project)
- `deadline` — When is it due?
- `contacts` — Who are the client's contacts?

## Suggested routines
- **Weekly client update** — every Friday, draft a status note from deliverables.md and the week's meetings for you to review and send.
- **Meeting follow-up** — after each meeting with the client, add its decisions to decisions.md and its actions to deliverables.md.
