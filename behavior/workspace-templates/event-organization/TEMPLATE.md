title: Event organization
description: Plan and run an event — venue, speakers, sponsors, attendees, budget and follow-up.
when: A meetup, workshop, conference track or launch with a date, a venue and people to coordinate.
---
## Creates
- README.md — what this workspace is, one paragraph
- overview.md — date, venue, capacity, goals, the people organizing
- run-of-show.md — the evening minute by minute
- speakers.md — who speaks, status, talk titles, what each needs
- sponsors-and-budget.md — sponsors, what they get, costs, invoices
- attendees-and-comms.md — RSVP numbers and the message plan before and after
- tasks.md — every task with an owner and a due date
- venue-logistics.md — access, AV, food, accessibility, the venue contact
- follow-up.md — what happens the week after

## Questions
- `event_name` — What is the event called? (default: Our event)
- `event_date` — When is it (date and start time)?
- `venue` — Where is it? (default: venue to confirm)
- `capacity` — How many people can attend?
- `organizers` — Who is organizing with you?

## Suggested routines
- **Weekly organizer digest** — every Monday, summarize open tasks, overdue owners, speaker and sponsor status into one message for the organizers.
- **RSVP watcher** — a sensor on the RSVP source (form, Luma, Meetup) that updates the count in attendees-and-comms.md and flags when you pass capacity.
