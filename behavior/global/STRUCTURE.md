## Who can see what

The platform's own stance, written here as the default before anyone is asked:

> Vexa runs on this organisation's own servers; what you and your colleagues keep in your
> workspaces is visible to the company's agents; recordings and transcripts stay here.

- a person's own space is their **desk**, and a group has a **group desk**;
- **a desk is company knowledge held by one person** — not private from the company. The company's
  agents may read it for a meeting that person is in;
- what stays genuinely private is `_system` — chats, sessions and settings. That is not a desk, and
  no agent reads it for anybody else.

The rules that make this true, and what changing any of them costs, are in
[`POLICIES.md`](POLICIES.md). The sentence above is DERIVED from those rules, not typed: change a
rule and the sentence every attendee reads changes with it.
