title: "Notifications"
description: "What tells you that someone added you to a chat, mentioned you, invited you to a workspace or started a call, and how those notices are kept private."
---

Vexa tells you when something happens to you that you did not do yourself: someone added you to a
chat or shared one with you, mentioned you, invited you to a workspace or added you to one, accepted
or declined your invite, started a call in a chat you are in, or a routine posted a run there.

## What you can do

### See a notification as it happens

A notice slides in at the top right and stays for six seconds. Up to three stack, newest on top.
Click it to open what it is about: the chat, the workspace, or the invite's join page. Click **×** to
dismiss it. Every tab you have open shows it at once.

### Read the list

The bell in the header of your chat list shows how many notices you have not read. Press it for the
list, newest first. Opening the list marks what it shows as read; **Mark all read** clears the rest.
Pressing a notice opens its object.

### Ask the agent

Ask *"what did I miss?"* in any chat. The agent reads the same list with `notifications_list` and
answers in a few lines, naming who and where. It can mark them read with `notifications_mark_read`
when you say you have seen them.

### Your agent can notify you

An agent working for you, in a chat or a routine's run, can post a notice with the `notify` tool:
one line naming what needs you, for example *3 emails need a reply today: the bank, the landlord, the
school*. A routine that says *notify me of important ones* uses it, so the bell lights up for important
mail. The notice opens the chat it came from, or a page or meeting the agent names. It goes to you;
in a shared chat the agent may notify the other people in that chat, never anyone else.

### Act on a notice in one tap

Each notice carries its first act as a button, in the toast and in the list:

| Notice | First act | Also |
|---|---|---|
| Someone invited you to a workspace | **Accept**: you join, and the workspace opens in your current chat | **Decline**: the invite stops working and its sender is told |
| A chat was shared with you, or you were added to one | **Open chat** | |
| Someone mentioned you | **Open chat** | |
| A meeting was shared with you | **Open transcript** | |
| A bot joined your meeting | **Open** | |
| A bot will join your calendar meeting | (nothing to open yet) | |

Acting marks the notice read in every tab you have open. An answered invite reads **Joined · Open**
or **Declined**. You can also ask the agent: *"accept the invite from Alice"* — it calls
`notifications_act` and confirms in one line.

### When the tab is in the background

The first time you open the bell, it offers one line: *Get an alert when this tab is in the
background?* **Turn on** asks your browser for permission once; **Not now** hides the offer for
good. With permission, a new notice that arrives while the tab is hidden shows as a browser
notification with a soft two-note chime; clicking it brings the tab forward and opens the notice.

### The list

Unread notices come first under **New**, the rest under **Earlier**, each with a relative time
("5 min ago"). An empty list says *You're all caught up*. The list fits a phone screen.

## Limits and errors

| You see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| no bell count and an empty list | the deployment has no session store, which keeps notifications | an operator turns the session store on ([Shared chats](/minutes/shared-chats#configuration)) |
| a notice arrives only after a reload | the live socket was down; notices are stored, so the list shows them on load | nothing: they are not lost |

Agent notices: one line of at most 200 characters; at most 20 an hour per person; a routine's run
posts at most one; the same text to the same person within ten minutes is posted once; a page link
goes only to people who can read the page, a meeting link to you only. Refusals are typed
(`rate_limited`, `one_per_run`, `not_a_participant`, `link_not_readable`, `agent_notifications_off`).
An operator turns agent notices off with `agent_notifications: off` in the front matter of
`_global/POLICIES.md` (default on).

Each person keeps their latest 500 notices; older read ones are dropped, unread ones never. A second
identical notice (same person, same chat, same kind) while the first is unread within ten minutes
refreshes the first instead of stacking.

## How it works

### One store, one writer per kind

Notices live in the session store's database, in the `agent_session_notification` table, one row per
recipient: who did it, what kind, which object, the sentence, when, and whether it was read. agent-api
is the only writer, through the store's own database role. Each kind is written by the route that does
the act, and nowhere else:

| Kind | Written by | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| added to a chat / a chat shared with you | `POST /api/sessions/{id}/participants` | the person added |
| mentioned | `POST /api/chat`, `/api/chat/submit` (a message's `mentions`) | each mentioned person already in the chat |
| a routine posted a run | the same routes, for a routine's scheduled run in a shared chat | everyone in it but the owner |
| a call started | `POST /api/sessions/{id}/call` | everyone in the chat but the caller |
| invited to a workspace | `POST /api/workspace/invite` (an address with an account here) | the invitee |
| added to a workspace | `POST /api/workspace/members/add` | the person added |
| your invite accepted / declined | `POST /api/workspace/invites/accept` / `…/decline` | whoever made the invite |
| your agent's own notice (kind `agent`) | `POST /api/notifications/notify` (the `notify` tool) | you, or people in the same chat it names |

### Delivery

Each new row is published on the recipient's own Redis channel, `u:{user_id}:notifications`. The
gateway's `/ws` socket subscribes every connection to its own person's channel when it opens, beside
the meeting-status channel, so each open tab receives it. The terminal shows the toast and updates the
bell. On load, and after a reconnect, the terminal reads `GET /api/notifications`. The same channel
carries `rail` frames, which tell your chat list to read itself again when a chat joins or leaves it;
these are not stored.

### Routes

| Browser calls (terminal) | agent-api (through the gateway's `/agent/*`) |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/notifications` (`unread`, `limit`, `before`) | the same path (also the agent tool `notifications_list`) |
| `POST /api/notifications/read` (`ids` or `all`) | the same path (also `notifications_mark_read`) |
| `POST /api/workspace/invites/decline` (`token`) | the same path |
| `POST /api/notifications/act` (`id`, `action`: `accept`, `decline`, `join`, `open`) | the same path (also `notifications_act`); a person verb, refused to a routine |
| — | `POST /api/notifications/notify` (`text`, `session`, `to`, `link`; the agent tool `notify`) |
| — | `POST /internal/notifications` (internal tier only): flows names a meeting fact, agent-api writes it |

The frames are `Notification`, `NotificationUpdate` (a notice acted on, in any tab), `NotificationRead`
and `Rail` in the `ws.v1` contract. Each row carries `actions` (what it offers now) and `state`
(`accepted`, `declined`, `joined`).

### Meeting notices

Three kinds come from facts flows holds: `meeting_shared` (flows mails a meeting's share link; the
account behind the address gets the same link as **Open transcript**), `bot_scheduled` (a calendar
invite set a bot to join) and `bot_joined` (the bot went live). flows names the fact on agent-api's
internal tier; agent-api composes the sentence and stores the row, so the store keeps one writer. An
address with no account is told by the mail alone. A refused or failed hand-over never fails the mail
or the bot.

### Configuration

Nothing new. Notifications are on wherever the session store is on; live delivery uses the Redis URL
agent-api already has. Upgrading adds one table and its index, created by the session store's
migrations step (`session_schema init`), which every deployment path already runs.

## Why it complies

### Architecture

| Rule | How this design meets it | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| **P2** contracts | the frames are in `ws.v1` with goldens; the routes are agent-api's own | `gate:schema`, `gate:contract-version` |
| **P20** authorize every access | every read and mark-read is keyed by the signed-in person; no parameter names another | deny tests below |
| **P23** one writer per carrier | agent-api is the only writer of the table and the channel; each kind has one emitting route | `gate:dataflow` |
| **P4** published schemas | `Notice` (with `actions` and `state`) and `NotificationUpdate` are in `ws.v1`, pinned by goldens; `notifications_act` is in `mcp.tools.v1` and `routes.v1` | `gate:schema`, `gate:contract-version` |
| **P20** person verbs | accept, decline and join from a notice are `person` verbs in `routes.v1`, refused to a routine by the app-wide gate; the internal door answers only the internal tier | deny tests below |
| **P21** state from evidence | a deployment that already had the table gains `source` and `acted` from the store's init step (`ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`, nullable), never from a guess at start-up | `core/agent/tests/test_session_schema.py` · `test_init_adds_the_columns_an_existing_table_lacks`, `test_a_sqlite_store_made_before_the_columns_gains_them` |
| **P18** fail loud, never fail the act | a notice that cannot be stored or published is logged; the act it reports has already happened and is never undone or refused for it | `test_a_failed_publish_keeps_the_row` |

### Security

**Who can do what.** You read, mark and act on only your own notices; another person's id answers
404. Nobody can write one except agent-api, as a side effect of an act it performed or of a meeting
fact flows handed it on the internal tier (meeting kinds only, a link only to a path in this app).
Accepting, declining and joining from a notice are the same acts as the join page and need you in the
loop: a routine cannot do them.

**Protections.**

- **A notice never reveals content you cannot read.** The sentence is composed by agent-api from two
  names only, the actor's and the object's (a chat's title, a workspace's name, a routine's name).
  There is no field that could carry a message, a page or a transcript line. A chat's title is named
  only to people who can read that chat; a mention of someone not in the chat tells them nothing.
- An agent's own notice is its one line, bounded to 200 characters, and goes only to its owner or to
  people in the same chat, whose shared turn reads only what every one of them can read. A page link
  is refused for anyone who cannot read the page.
- A workspace invite's notice holds its join link. The link is restricted to the invitee's verified
  address, so it grants nobody else anything, and only the invitee can read the row.
- Only the person an invite names can decline it; any other token gets the same answer as an unknown
  one.
- The socket subscribes to its own person's channel, resolved from the key at connect. A client cannot
  name another channel.
