---
title: "Voice notes"
description: "Record a note in Minutes with no meeting to join; it is transcribed live and kept as a meeting."
---

A voice note is a recording you make in Minutes, with no meeting platform involved. It is transcribed
as you speak, by the same pipeline a meeting's bot uses, and it is kept as a meeting: the meeting
page, the transcript, notes, search, sharing and export all work on it unchanged.

## What you can do

### Record

1. Start it one of two ways:
   - in a new, empty chat, press the suggestion **Record a voice note**; or
   - ask the agent, in any chat: *"record a voice note"*. The agent calls its `record_voice_note` tool,
     which opens the recorder in this tab. The agent cannot hear or record you: the microphone is
     yours, and the note is what you say into it.

   The browser asks for the microphone the first time.
2. The note opens as its own meeting, titled *Voice note*. Its page header shows *Recording m:ss / max*
   with a level dot, **Pause** and **Stop**; the chats rail shows a running clock too.
3. Speak. The transcript fills in as you talk, under your own name, and while the note records it is
   most of the page: it wraps, follows the newest line, and counts the line still being said. The
   notes are written after you stop, the way a meeting's are after it ends.

The microphone in the chat composer is dictation: it types what you say into the message box, and
makes no voice note.

### Pause, resume and stop

- **Pause** stops sending audio without ending the note; **Resume** continues the same note.
- **Stop** (on the meeting page, or the clock in the rail) sends what is left of the audio, then ends
  the note. The bot finishes the transcript and the note becomes a finished meeting.
- Closing the tab without Stop ends the note after two minutes of silence (`VOICE_NOTE_IDLE_TIMEOUT_SEC`);
  what was sent is kept. A paused note stays open while the tab is open, but not past the limit below:
  the clock keeps running while you are paused.

### Limits

A note can last up to 30 minutes of audio (`VOICE_NOTE_MAX_DURATION_SEC`). At the limit the note is
saved and ended for you (*The note reached its maximum length and was saved.*). Paused time counts
too: a note ends at most a minute after `VOICE_NOTE_MAX_DURATION_SEC` of wall-clock time, however much
of it was paused. A voice note counts toward your number of concurrent bots.

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Microphone access was refused…* | the browser denied the microphone | allow it in the browser's site settings, then record again |
| *Your key cannot record voice notes (it needs the bot scope).* | your sign-in key has no `bot` scope | ask your administrator |
| *This voice note has already ended.* | the note was stopped elsewhere, or timed out | start a new note |
| *The connection to the recorder closed. What was sent is kept.* | the network dropped | start a new note; the transcript so far stays |
| *Your sign-in was not accepted for recording. Sign out, sign in again, and retry.* | the recorder refused the sign-in the page sent | sign out and in again |
| *Could not reach the recorder. Nothing was saved; try again.* | the recorder did not answer within 10 seconds | record again |
| *No audio from your microphone…* (while recording) | the microphone has delivered nothing for 5 seconds | unmute it or close the app using it; the note keeps running |

These messages show on the note's meeting page. A note that could not start at all (the microphone
refused, for example) shows *Not recording* in the chats rail; hover it for the reason, press it to
dismiss it. When a note ends on the server
(Stop on another device, the idle rule, the limit) while this tab is still recording it, the page lets
the microphone go.

## How it works

### Flow

1. **Open the recorder.** The empty chat's *Record a voice note* suggestion, or the agent's
   `record_voice_note` tool, raises one event in the tab, and the recorder starts. The tool is
   `POST /voice-notes/recorder` on meeting-api (scope `bot`): it creates nothing and answers
   `{"status": "awaiting_user", "ui_action": "open_voice_note", "instruction": …}`; the terminal acts on
   the tool call the way it does for `connection_request`. In a run with nobody present (a routine, a
   background flow, a meeting run) the tool is refused with `human_session_required`.
2. **Start.** The terminal posts `POST /api/bots {"platform": "voice_note"}`. meeting-api makes the
   meeting row (`vn-…` id) and spawns a bot whose invocation has no meeting URL and a `voiceNote` block:
   the note's audio stream, its cap and idle timeout.
3. **Capture.** The browser captures the microphone with `getUserMedia` and an `AudioWorklet`,
   resamples to 16 kHz mono, and cuts 100 ms capture.v1 audio frames, each stamped with its capture
   time (the same frame the extension and the bot use).
4. **Stream.** The frames go over the same-origin socket `/voice-notes/{id}/stream`. The terminal
   server takes the upgrade only from its own origin and only with your session cookie, adds your key
   from that cookie and relays the socket to the gateway, which authorizes the upgrade (scope `bot`,
   and your per-user request budget), signs your identity onto the hop and relays it to meeting-api.
5. **Buffer.** meeting-api checks the note is yours and live, validates each frame, counts the audio
   against the cap and appends the frame to the note's Redis stream. Audio is taken at up to 1.5×
   realtime after a 5-second burst; a faster sender waits. Audio sent while the bot is still starting
   waits there.
6. **Transcribe.** The bot reads the stream in order and feeds the frames to the same per-speaker lane,
   speech-to-text service and transcript and lifecycle sinks a meeting's audio takes. It joins
   nothing and launches no browser.
7. **Stop.** Stop appends an end marker. The bot drains what is left, flushes the last words and
   reports the meeting `completed`, as at the end of a meeting.

### The socket

`WS /voice-notes/{native_meeting_id}/stream` (through the gateway; a plain `GET` answers `426`).

| Direction | Frame |
|---|---|
| server | `{"type":"ready","max_duration_sec":N,"received_sec":s}` |
| client | binary capture.v1 audio frame: Int32 track, Float64 capture epoch ms, mono 16 kHz Float32 PCM, at most one second |
| server | `{"type":"progress","received_sec":s}` about once a second of audio |
| client → server | `{"type":"ping"}` → `{"type":"pong"}`, every 15 s; keeps a paused note open (at most one keepalive is stored per 5 s) |
| client → server | `{"type":"stop"}` → `{"type":"stopped","received_sec":s}`, then close `1000` |

Refusals are an `{"type":"error","error":…}` frame, then a close: `missing_api_key` / `invalid_api_key`
4401, `insufficient_scope` 4403, `rate_limited` 4429, `auth_unavailable` 4503, `upstream_unavailable`
4502, `frame_too_large` 4400 for a frame over 128 KiB (at the gateway); `not_found` 4404, `bad_frame`
4400, `max_duration` 4413, `rate_limited` 4429 (text frames past a budget of 20, refilled 2 a second)
(at meeting-api). A text frame meeting-api does not recognise is answered with
`{"type":"error","error":"unknown_message"}` and the socket stays open. The terminal server refuses an
upgrade from another origin (`403`) and a voice-note upgrade without a session cookie (`401`). A
reconnect to a live note continues it; the audio count survives.

### Who writes what

| Data | Single writer | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The note's audio stream (`voice_note:meeting:{id}:audio`) | meeting-api | Redis; see *Retention* |
| Transcript segments | the bot, then meeting-api | the transcript stream in Redis, then Postgres |
| Meeting row and status | meeting-api | Postgres |

### Configuration

| Key | Service | Default | Compose (`deploy/compose/.env`) | Helm value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `VOICE_NOTE_MAX_DURATION_SEC` | meeting-api | `1800` | `VOICE_NOTE_MAX_DURATION_SEC=1800` | `meetingApi.voiceNoteMaxDurationSeconds` |
| `VOICE_NOTE_IDLE_TIMEOUT_SEC` | meeting-api | `120` | `VOICE_NOTE_IDLE_TIMEOUT_SEC=120` | `meetingApi.voiceNoteIdleTimeoutSeconds` |

On Compose, set the key in `deploy/compose/.env` and apply it with `docker compose up -d meeting-api`
(a `restart` does not re-read `.env`). On Helm, set the value and run `helm upgrade`. meeting-api reads
both keys at each note's start and hands the bot its cap and idle timeout in the invocation.

No recording file is kept for a voice note; the transcript is the record.

### Retention

The raw audio exists only in the note's Redis stream, and only as long as the bot needs it:

- while the note runs, the stream and its audio count expire 10 minutes after the last write (or
  twice `VOICE_NOTE_IDLE_TIMEOUT_SEC`, whichever is longer);
- after Stop or the cap, they expire 5 minutes after the end marker;
- when the note completes or fails, meeting-api deletes them;
- deleting the meeting deletes them too, if anything is left.

## Why it complies

### Architecture

| Rule | How this design meets it | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| **P2** contracts | a voice note is the sealed `invocation.v1` / `api.v1` / `ws.v1` platform value `voice_note`; the audio is capture.v1 frames; the socket is a declared `routes.v1` meetings row | `gate:schema`, `gate:contract-version` |
| **P7** disposable workers | the bot is the ordinary meeting bot, without its browser; it boots, drains, emits, dies | bot `voice-note.test.ts` |
| **P18** fail loud | every refusal is a typed close with a reason the terminal turns into words | meeting-api `test_voice_note.py`, gateway `test_voice_relay.py` |
| **P20** authorize every access | the gateway checks the key and its scope at the upgrade; meeting-api checks the signed identity owns the note | the deny tests below |
| **P22** guaranteed teardown | Stop ends the note; a vanished browser ends it on the idle timeout; the bot cannot outlive the cap by more than a minute | bot `voice-note.test.ts` |
| **P23** one writer per carrier | meeting-api alone writes the audio stream; the bot alone reads it | `bot_spawn.workload_redis` ACL rules |

### Security

**Who can do what.** Only the note's owner can stream into it, and only with a key that has the `bot`
scope. A worker's delegation token cannot open the socket. The agent's `record_voice_note` tool only
asks the person's open tab to start the recorder: it creates no note, carries no audio, and is
refused to a worker running with nobody present.

**Protections.**

- The identity on the hop is the gateway's signed identity; meeting-api refuses an unsigned one.
- The bot's own Redis user may read its note's audio stream and nothing else: a read-only selector
  for that one key, so it can never read the shared transcript stream.
- Frames are validated (shape, size, capture time) before they are stored; nothing else is accepted.
- The note's length is capped across reconnects, and audio is taken at no more than 1.5× realtime.
  Keepalives and text frames are budgeted. The stream is deleted when the note ends and expires
  minutes after its last write.
- The terminal server takes the socket only from its own origin, and only with your session cookie;
  a voice note never falls back to a deployment key.
- The browser never holds your key: the terminal server adds it.

### End to end

`core/meetings/eval/voice-note-e2e/run.sh` streams a synthetic speech WAV through this socket to a real
bot and speech-to-text service. On 2026-10-12: 34.89 s sent, 34.89 s acknowledged, 34.14 s
transcribed in 5 segments, every sentence returned in order, the note `completed` one second after
Stop.
