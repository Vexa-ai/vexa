A **workspace template** is a set of pages a new workspace starts with, plus the questions to ask
before creating it. Vexa ships four:

| Template | Pages it creates | Asks |
|---|---|---|
| **Event organization** | overview, run of show, speakers, sponsors & budget, attendees & comms, tasks, venue logistics, follow-up | name, date, venue, capacity, organizers |
| **Client project** | overview, meetings, decisions, deliverables, risks | client, project, deadline, contacts |
| **Meeting series / community** | overview, members, notes, decisions, next agenda | name, cadence, purpose |
| **Research** | question, sources, interviews, findings, answer | question, decision it informs, due date |

Each also lists **suggested routines**, for example a weekly organizer digest or an RSVP watcher for
an event. They are suggestions: nothing is switched on until you ask for it.

## What you can do

- **Create from a template.** Say *"create a workspace for our meetup from the event template"*.
  The agent asks the template's questions in one message, creates the workspace with your answers
  filled in, and the chat writes to it from then on. An unanswered question is marked *to decide*
  in the pages.
- **Create a shared workspace from an empty chat.** The **Create a shared workspace** starter asks
  what it is for, picks a template, creates it, makes an invite link (or offers to share by email),
  and offers a calendar event that carries the link. Nobody is invited until you say yes.
- **Save a workspace as a template.** Say *"save this workspace as a template called board meeting"*.
  Its pages (not its records or history) become your own template, offered from then on.
- **Change the company's templates.** As the [admin account](/minutes/admin-account), ask in its
  chat. The company's templates are edited there and every change can be undone from the history.

| Message | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *no workspace template called 'x'* | the name is not a template you can use | the message lists the ones there are |
| *you already have a template called 'x'* | saving over your own template | say replace, or pick another name |
| *the admin account works on the company layer only* | the admin account has no workspaces | sign in with your everyday account |

## How it works

1. **Where templates live.** The company's are in `_global/workspace-templates/<name>/`; yours are
   in your Personal workspace under `templates/workspaces/<name>/`. Yours win when the names match.
   Each is a `TEMPLATE.md` manifest (title, description, when to use it, `## Creates`,
   `## Questions`, `## Suggested routines`) plus the pages; a page may carry a placeholder for each
   answer.
2. **Seeds.** The four built-in templates ship in the agent-api image (`behavior/workspace-templates`)
   and are copied into `_global/workspace-templates/` at boot when missing. A copy that is already
   there is never overwritten, so an edit by the admin account survives every upgrade.
3. **Create.** `workspace_create` runs the same create as `workspace_new`, then writes the template's
   pages with the answers filled in and commits them in the new workspace. The answer names the
   pages, the questions left unanswered, and the suggested routines.
4. **Save.** `workspace_template_save` copies a workspace's pages (not `kg/entities/`, history or
   settings) into your Personal workspace and commits them there.

| Tool | Route (agent-api) | Who |
|---|---|---|
| `workspace_templates_list` | `GET /api/workspace/templates` | any signed-in person |
| `workspace_create` | `POST /api/workspace/create` | any signed-in person; refused to the admin account |
| `workspace_template_save` | `POST /api/workspace/templates/save` | any signed-in person, into their own Personal; refused to the admin account |

### Who writes what, and configuration

| Data | Written by |
|---|---|
| `_global/workspace-templates/` | the seed at boot (missing files only) and the admin account |
| `templates/workspaces/` in a Personal workspace | `workspace_template_save`, for that person only |
| a new workspace's pages | `workspace_create`, once, in the new workspace |

The image's seed directory is `/app/behavior/workspace-templates`; `VEXA_WORKSPACE_TEMPLATES_DIR`
points it elsewhere. With no company templates in `_global`, the list falls back to the image's copy.

### Security

**Who can do what.** Any signed-in person lists templates and creates a workspace from one, which
starts private and theirs. Saving reads a workspace the person can read and writes only into their
own Personal workspace. The admin account is refused both create and save. An agent acting for a
person is held to its chat's workspace scope when it names a workspace to save.

**Protections.**

- Answers are capped in length, and template markers inside an answer are removed before they are
  filled in.
- Template pages are read and written through the workspace path guard, so a template cannot write
  outside the new workspace.
- Saving skips entity records, history, settings, flows and skills.
