A meeting in Minutes is a chat bound to that meeting, with its pages beside it. Before and during the
meeting the page is the **Brief**; afterwards it is the **Minutes**. A **Transcript** tab shows the
words as they are said. This page covers what you can do with a meeting from the moment the bot is sent.

## What you can do

### Send a bot

In an empty chat, press **Paste a meeting link** (or paste one) and ask the agent to join. When the bot
is sent, the chat binds to that meeting and its pages are pinned. A meeting you start inside a shared
workspace belongs to that workspace, so its members can open it too; a meeting from your desk stays
yours. Meetings also arrive by calendar invitation. See [Send a bot](/how-to/send-a-bot) for platforms
and links.

### Follow it live

- The page header shows the meeting's title and one metadata line: platform, start time, status and
  the number of participants.
- A state card says what the bot is doing: *Bot at the door*, *Bot needs someone to let it in*,
  *Bot admitted · no words for …*, *Reconnecting to the bot…*, *Bot left*, or *Bot feed error*.
- The transcript fills in as people talk (*Nothing said yet — this fills in as the room talks.*).
  Opening a meeting part-way through replays everything said so far, then continues live.
- **Stop bot** takes the bot out of the meeting. It is shown while the bot is requested, joining,
  waiting, active or stopping, and only to the meeting's owner.

### Highlight

Press **Highlight** on the transcript. The agent picks out the people, companies and projects the
meeting has named and marks them on the transcript; nothing is posted to the chat. Pressing it again
adds only what was said since (*Highlight · N* shows how many terms there are).

- A **solid** term has a page: click to open it.
- A **dashed** term has none yet: click (*Find out what … is*) and the chat looks it up.

Highlight works on live and finished meetings.

### Ask about a passage

Select text in the transcript and press **Ask about this** (*Add this quote to your chat*). The quote,
with the meeting it came from, is drafted into the chat box. Nothing is sent until you send it.

To bring a whole meeting page up to date, use **Extend this meeting page** under the page; it reads only
what was said since the last extend. See [Pages](/minutes/pages#extend-or-create-a-page-with-the-agent).

### Copy the transcript

**Copy transcript** copies every confirmed line with its speaker; see
[Manage your chats](/how-to/chat-controls#copy-a-meeting-transcript).

### Play the recording

On a finished meeting, the controls load the recording (*Loading recording…*). The player is one row
under the page's title: the play/pause icon, the time (*0:00 / 51:13*), the **Seek recording** slider
filling the rest of the row, and the download icon. On a narrow panel the slider shrinks; the row stays
on one line. There is no mute: use your system volume.

1. Press the play icon (*Play recording*; it turns into *Pause recording*). Nothing plays by itself.
2. Click any transcript passage, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> on it, to jump there.
   Hovering a passage shows *Play from m:ss* just above that line. The passage being played is shaded.
3. Drag the **Seek recording** slider to move anywhere in the audio.

There is one player per audio file, and starting one meeting pauses any other. Older recordings have
no exact start mark, so timing uses the meeting's start and may be off by a little (the controls say
so).

### Download the audio

Press the download icon at the end of the player row (*Download audio*). The browser saves the
recording as `<platform>-<meeting-id>-<date>.<ext>`, in its own format (usually `.webm`). It goes
through the same owner-only check as playback. If the download is refused or interrupted, the
controls say so under the player (*Could not download audio…*). Over the API, see
[Recordings](/how-to/recordings).

### Delete a meeting's data

On a finished meeting, the owner sees the trash icon (*Delete meeting data*) with the other icons in the
page header (copy, report, edit). It turns red only when you point at it.

1. Press it. The dialog reads *Delete meeting data?*: *Permanently delete this meeting's audio,
   transcript and captured fixtures, including promoted fixtures. Saved pages are kept.*
2. Type `delete`. **Delete permanently** stays disabled until you do.
3. When it is done: *Meeting audio, transcript and fixtures deleted. Saved pages are kept.*

The meeting stays in your list, marked as deleted, and its transcript says *This meeting's transcript
and recording were deleted.* Pages written from it are not touched.

### Open a meeting by link

`/?meeting=<platform>/<meeting-id>` (or a meeting's row number) opens that meeting's room. Add
`&view=transcript`, `minutes`, `brief` or `file:<path>` to choose what is in front; see
[Links into Minutes](/minutes/overview#links-into-minutes).

## Limits and errors

| You see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *No audio recording available.* | the meeting has no recording, or you are a workspace member rather than the owner: recordings are served to the meeting's owner only | ask the owner |
| *Could not load recording* · *Audio could not be played. Retry loading the recording.* | the recording could not be fetched or played | press **Retry** |
| *Could not stop bot. Retry.* | the stop request failed | retry; the bot is also ended when the meeting ends |
| *Could not delete meeting data. Retry.* | the delete failed; storage may be unavailable | retry later |
| *Could not download audio: the recording was not found.* · *Could not download audio (HTTP …). Retry.* · *…the connection failed. Retry.* | the download was refused or did not complete | press the download icon again; a recording you do not own is never served |
| *This page declares a transcript, but names no meeting.* | a page has a transcript slot without a meeting id | fix the slot or ask the agent |
| *This meeting's transcript and recording were deleted.* | the meeting's data was deleted | — |
| *Bot feed error* · *Unreadable transcript frame* | the live feed failed or sent something unreadable | the card reconnects; dismiss it |

Delete is offered only after a meeting has finished; the server refuses it while the meeting is still
running. Stop and Delete are not shown on a meeting someone shared with you.

## How it works

### Flow

1. **List and open.** The terminal's catch-all proxy sends `/api/meetings` and
   `/api/transcripts/by-id/{id}` to meeting-api through the gateway. The meeting's page path comes from
   agent-api (`/api/meeting/note`), which creates the page if it is missing and never overwrites one.
2. **Live transcript.** The browser opens `/api/meeting/stream?meeting_id=…&session_uid=…` as
   server-sent events. agent-api checks that you may read the meeting, then reads the meeting's
   transcript stream: from the start on a fresh connection, then live. A reconnect resumes from the last
   event it saw.
3. **Playback.** The controls list recordings at `/api/recordings?meeting_id=…` and stream
   `/api/recordings/{id}/media/{media}/raw?type=audio`. The terminal passes byte ranges through, so the
   player can seek; meeting-api serves the ranges from object storage. Download reads the same route
   with `download=1`, which adds `Content-Disposition: attachment`. A transcript line's play position
   is its timestamp minus the recording's capture start.
4. **Highlight.** The button sends a silent `highlight` request into the chat. The agent scans the
   transcript for candidates and publishes the ones it keeps; the terminal reads them from
   `/api/meeting/terms`. Terms are added, never overwritten.
5. **Delete.** `DELETE /api/meetings/{id}` reaches meeting-api, which deletes the stored objects first,
   then the fixtures, then the transcript rows and the meeting's data keys, and marks the meeting as
   deleted.
6. **Stop.** `DELETE /api/bots/{platform}/{meeting-id}` reaches meeting-api's stop route.

### Who writes what

| Data | Single writer | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting rows | meeting-api | Postgres |
| Transcript segments | meeting-api | Postgres, and the meeting's transcript stream in Redis (agent-api and the terminal only read it) |
| Recording master | meeting-api (from the bot's chunks) | object storage |
| Meeting pages | agent-api | the workspace's git repository |
| Highlight terms | agent-api | `.vexa/meeting-terms/<meeting>.json` on the reader's desk |

### Routes

| Browser calls (terminal) | Goes to |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/meetings`, `GET /api/transcripts/by-id/{id}` | meeting-api, through the gateway |
| `GET /api/recordings`, `GET /api/recordings/{id}/media/{media}/raw` (with `Range`) | meeting-api, through the gateway |
| `DELETE /api/meetings/{id}`, `DELETE /api/bots/{platform}/{id}` | meeting-api, through the gateway |
| `GET /api/meeting/stream` (server-sent events) | agent-api `/api/meeting/stream`, through the gateway's `/agent/*` |
| `GET`, `POST /api/meeting/note`; `GET /api/meeting/terms` | agent-api, through the gateway's `/agent/*` |

The terminal decides the destination by path: `meetings`, `transcripts`, `bots`, `recordings` and `user`
go to the meetings side, everything else to the agent. The meetings routes are documented in the
[Meetings API](/api/meetings).

### Configuration

`GATEWAY_URL` on the terminal server. Recording storage is configured on meeting-api; see
[Recordings](/how-to/recordings).

## Why it complies

### Architecture

| Rule | How this design meets it | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| **P2** contracts · **P6** one front door | the browser calls only the terminal; meeting routes are meeting-api's sealed `api.v1` | `gate:isolation`, `gate:exports`, `gate:contract-conformance` |
| **P18** fail loud | an unreachable gateway is a `502`; a stream failure arrives as a `stream-error` event; a recording that cannot be made seekable is a typed fault; each control says when it failed | ungated: reviewed |
| **P20** authorize every access | agent-api and meeting-api check every read: the owner, a share recipient, or a member of the meeting's workspace; recordings and delete are owner-only | `gate:access` and the deny tests below |
| **P21** state from evidence | the bot card reports what the bot is observed doing, including *admitted · no words for …* | ungated: reviewed |
| **P22** guaranteed teardown | **Stop bot** uses meeting-api's stop route, which ends the bot directly when it cannot confirm the bot is listening | the meeting-lifecycle eval under `gate:python` |
| **P23** one writer per carrier | meeting-api is the only writer of the transcript stream; agent-api and the terminal read it, and the terminal renders it with one engine | `gate:dataflow` |

### Security

**Who can do what.** You can open a meeting you own, a meeting shared with you, and a meeting that
belongs to a workspace you are a member of. Only the owner can play the recording, stop the bot or
delete the meeting's data.

**Protections.**

- The user is the gateway-signed identity. Workspace membership is read from the workspace's roster,
  never from a request header.
- A meeting you may not read is refused before the live stream opens, and a stream for someone else's
  bot session is refused.
- Someone who is not the owner gets the same `404` as for a meeting that does not exist, so meetings
  cannot be discovered.
- A deleted meeting's transcript is refused, and new segments for it are refused.
- Recording responses are `private, no-store`. A bot-only API key cannot subscribe to a transcript.
- Meeting ids in links are checked so they cannot escape their path segment.
