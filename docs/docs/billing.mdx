---
title: "Billing & credit balance"
description: "How the credit balance, plan fees, top-ups, and per-minute usage billing work on vexa.ai"
---

Billing on [vexa.ai](https://vexa.ai) has two separate money surfaces. Knowing
which one a charge touches removes almost every surprise:

1. **Credit balance** — prepaid credit that pays for *usage*.
2. **Your card** — pays for *plan fees* directly.

## Credit balance

Your credit balance (Account → Balance) is everything Vexa owes you in
credit:

- **Top-up and welcome credit** — the welcome credit plus any Add Funds
  top-ups. Spent **only on metered usage** (bot minutes and Vexa-served
  transcription minutes), applied automatically when usage is invoiced.
- **Refund credit** — e.g. the automatically prorated refund for unused plan
  time when you switch plans. This part applies to **your next invoice of any
  kind**, including plan fees.

New charges never draw from your balance directly: plan/subscription fees and
one-off invoices charge your saved payment method, and any refund credit you
hold is then applied against them automatically.

<Note>
If you subscribe to a plan and your credit balance does not change — that is
expected. Plan fees and credit balance are separate by design.
</Note>

## Top-ups

**Add Funds** (Account → Balance) charges your card or Link wallet once and
adds the amount to your credit balance as a paid credit. Minimum top-up is $2.

## Usage pricing

Usage is billed **per minute, rounded up once per meeting** (not per minute
of the meeting).

On **Pay-as-you-go**:

| Meter | Rate |
|---|---|
| Bot runtime | $0.30 / bot hour |
| Vexa-served real-time transcription | +$0.20 / hour |
| Your own transcription endpoint | no Vexa transcription charge |

The **Individual** plan ($12/mo) includes one concurrent bot with real-time
transcription; its monthly fee is charged to your card.

## Switching plans

Plan switches take effect immediately and start a **new billing period**:

- Unused time on the old plan is returned automatically as prorated Stripe
  credit toward your next invoices.
- Usage details in the Account page show the current billing period; usage
  recorded under a previous plan's period may not be listed there, but it
  remains recorded and billed correctly.

## Where to check what happened

- **Account → Balance** — current credit and the running usage total for the
  period.
- **Account → Billing History** — every payment, credit, and invoice, newest
  first.
- **Manage billing** — the Stripe portal for payment methods and invoices.
