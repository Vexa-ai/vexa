The left column of the terminal is one list of your chats. A meeting is a chat too: opening its row
opens the meeting's page. This page covers the controls on that list and on the chat header.

| Control | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Activity dot | each row | blue while the agent is working in that chat |
| Rename | the chat's name in the header | gives the chat your own title |
| Pin / Unpin | `⋯` on a row → **Pin to top** | keeps the row at the top, on every device |
| Hide / Unhide | `⋯` on a row → **Hide**; **Hidden** in the header to bring it back | takes the row out of the list, reversibly |
| Reorder pins | drag a pinned row onto another pinned row | saves the order of your pins |
| History | click a row | reopens the conversation |
| Copy transcript | the transcript on a meeting page | copies what was said |
| Delete | `⋯` on a row → **Delete…**, then `✓` | deletes the chat and its conversation for good |

## The order of the list

Pinned rows come first. Every other row follows, newest first: a chat by its last message, a meeting
by the later of its start and its chat's last message. A meeting that is live right now is lifted to
the top of the unpinned rows. A chat you start appears at the top of the unpinned rows.

## See which chats are working

A row shows a blue dot ("Agent is working") while a turn is running in that chat, or while a
background job started from it is running. A job that is only queued does not count. Several chats
can be working at once, and switching to another chat does not stop a turn.

Other dots: a live meeting's row has an accent dot and shows the time in the accent colour, and a
finished meeting's chat is tagged `held`.

**Limit.** The dot reflects the turns and jobs this browser tab is following. It is not read from the
server, so another browser or a reload does not show turns that tab is not streaming.

## Rename a chat

1. Open the chat. Its name is in the header, after `Chat ›` (or `Meeting ›`).
2. Click the name. It becomes a text box with **Save** and **Cancel**.
3. Type a title and press Enter or **Save**. **Escape** or **Cancel** leaves it unchanged.

- Titles are 1 to 100 characters. Spaces are collapsed and an empty title cannot be saved.
- A chat you have not sent anything in yet cannot be renamed.
- If the save fails, the box stays open with "Couldn't save. Try again."

The agent also names chats. Once a task is clear, it gives the chat a short title of three to seven
words with its `chat_name` tool. **A title you chose is never overwritten by the agent.** The list
picks up new names about every five seconds.

## Pin a row

1. Hover a row, or tab to it: a `⋯` button appears at its right (on a touch screen it is always
   there). Press it, or press Enter on it.
2. Choose **Pin to top**. The row moves to the top and shows a small pin.

Choose **Unpin** to put it back in time order. Pins are saved for your account, so they are the same
in every browser you sign in from and survive a reload. You can pin a chat or a meeting you can see,
including a meeting someone shared with you; your pins never change anyone else's list.

To reorder your pins, drag one pinned row onto another. Unpinned rows cannot be dragged: their place
is their time. If a pin or the order cannot be saved, the list keeps what it had and shows an error.

## Hide a row

Choose **Hide** in the row's `⋯` menu. The row leaves the list, from every device. Hiding deletes
nothing.

To bring it back, press **Hidden N** in the list's header. The list shows only hidden rows; choose
**Unhide** in a row's menu, then **Done**. **Hidden** appears only while something is hidden.

The menu works from the keyboard: the first item takes focus, Arrow Up and Down move, Enter chooses,
Escape closes it and returns focus to `⋯`.

## Reopen a chat's history

Click a row to reopen the conversation. The terminal loads its turns from the server, so the history
is there in any browser. Turns the product composed for you (for example from an emailed link) are
not shown as your messages. If loading fails, the chat opens empty and tries again the next time you
open it.

## Copy a meeting transcript

On a meeting page, the transcript has a **Copy transcript** button.

- It copies every finished segment, plus the one still being spoken, as the speaker's name on one
  line and the text below it, with a blank line between segments.
- It is disabled until someone has said something.
- After a copy it reads **Copied**. If the browser refuses the copy, it shows "Could not copy
  transcript".

The copy happens in your browser from the transcript already on the page. Nothing is sent to the
server.

## Delete a chat

1. In the row's `⋯` menu, choose **Delete…**. The `⋯` turns into a red `✓` ("Click again to delete
   chat").
2. Click `✓` to delete.

Escape, clicking anywhere else or moving focus away cancels it. A meeting row with no chat has no
**Delete…**: deleting a meeting is done on the meeting, not in this list.

**What delete does.** It is permanent and applies on every device:

| | After delete |
|---|---|
| The chat in your list | gone, everywhere |
| Its conversation (the turns you and the agent wrote) | deleted from the server; its history can no longer be opened |
| Pages and files it wrote into a workspace | **kept** — they belong to the workspace |
| Its pin, its hide and its place in your pin order | removed |
| A meeting the chat was about | **not deleted**. Its row is hidden for you, so the meeting does not reappear in the chat's place; unhide it from **Hidden** |
| A turn that is running in it | finishes in its worker; nothing shows it |

A chat that still has messages waiting to run cannot be deleted yet ("This chat still has queued
messages…"). Wait for them, then delete. A chat you never sent anything in exists only in your
browser, and delete removes it there. If you delete the chat you are in, the terminal opens your most
recent other chat, or a new one.

Before this release, delete only removed a chat from the list in one browser. The first time you sign
in after it, those chats are hidden for your account instead, so they stay out of your list
everywhere and can be brought back from **Hidden**.

## How it works

1. **The list.** The terminal calls `GET /api/sessions` and builds the rows from your session index,
   merged with your meetings. It refreshes every five seconds, so names the agent sets appear.
2. **Every call goes through the gateway.** The browser calls the terminal's own `/api/*` routes. The
   terminal forwards them to the gateway's `/agent/*` prefix with your key, and the gateway forwards
   them to agent-api's `/api/*` with a signed identity. The browser never sends a user id.
3. **Rename.** `POST /api/chat/name` with `{session, title}`. agent-api checks the title, checks the
   session is in your index (`404` if not) and stores it as a human name.
4. **Agent naming.** Each chat turn tells the agent its session id. The agent's `chat_name` tool calls
   `POST /agent/chat/name/agent` through the MCP re-entry route. That route always stores an agent
   name and has no field to claim a human one.
5. **Order.** The terminal sorts rows newest first and puts your pinned rows on top
   (`clients/terminal/src/minutes/chatOrder.ts`). `GET /api/chat/order` reads the saved order of
   your pins; `PUT /api/chat/order` with `{order}` replaces it. Each entry is a row key:
   `c:<chat session>` or `m:<meeting id>` (a bare session id is accepted too), the part after the
   prefix held to the session-name rule.
6. **Pin and hide.** `GET /api/chat/marks` returns `{pinned, hidden}`, lists of row keys.
   `POST /api/chat/marks` with `{row, pinned?, hidden?}` sets or clears one row's marks. To set a
   mark, agent-api checks you can see the row: a chat must be in your session index (or be a
   meeting's own chat, `meet-<id>`, on a meeting you can read); a meeting must pass the same access
   check that guards its transcript (owner, share recipient, or member of its workspace). The marks
   are stored under your own subject, so a shared meeting's owner never sees your pins or hides.
7. **Delete.** `DELETE /api/sessions/{session}` (the route the sealed `api.v1` contract declares).
   agent-api checks the session is in your index, refuses while its inbox has queued submissions,
   deletes the transcript and continuity pointer from your own workspace roots, drops the index
   entry, your marks and your order entry for it, and hides the meeting's row when the chat was a
   meeting's.
8. **History.** `GET /api/sessions/{session}/history` reads the conversation from the harness's
   transcript in your workspace and returns simplified turns.
9. **Activity and copy** run in the browser only. Activity is kept in the page's memory and copy
   uses the clipboard. The old browser-only delete list (`vexa.minutes.chatsHidden`) is read once,
   turned into hides through `POST /api/chat/marks`, and cleared.

**Who stores what.**

| Data | Writer | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Session index: titles, `named_title`, `name_source` | agent-api | Redis hash per session (`_Sessions` in `core/agent/control_plane/api_shared.py`), in memory without Redis |
| Rail order (of your pins) | agent-api | Redis key `agent:rail-order:{subject}`, a JSON list |
| Pins and hides | agent-api | Redis hash `agent:rail-marks:{subject}`, one field per mark (`p:<row>`, `h:<row>`) |
| Conversation | the worker's agent harness | the session transcript under the workspace's `.claude/projects/` |
| The "All" filter, collapsed columns | the terminal, in the browser | local storage |

**Routes.** `POST /api/chat/name`, `POST /api/chat/name/agent`, `GET` and `PUT /api/chat/order`,
`GET` and `POST /api/chat/marks`, `GET /api/sessions`, `DELETE /api/sessions/{session}`,
`GET /api/sessions/{session}/history`, all in `core/agent/control_plane/routers/chats.py`. The agent
route is declared in `core/agent/routes.v1.json` and in the `chat_name` row of
`core/agent/mcp.tools.v1.json`; the delete is a `person: true` verb there.

**Configuration.** None. These controls have no settings.

## Why it complies

**Architecture**

| Rule | How this design meets it | Enforced by |
|---|---|---|
| P6 — one front door | The browser reaches agent-api only through the terminal's `/api/*` proxy and the gateway. | `gate:domain-doors` |
| P4 — sealed contracts | The agent's naming tool is a row in `mcp.tools.v1.json`; its route is a row in `routes.v1.json`. Delete is the `DELETE /api/sessions/{session_id}` the sealed `api.v1` already declares, and a `person: true` verb in `routes.v1.json`. | `gate:schema`, `gate:contract-version`, `gate:contract-conformance`; `route_policy` refuses to start agent-api with an unflagged DELETE |
| P23 — one writer per carrier | agent-api alone writes the session index, the rail order and the pins and hides. The rail order and the marks are declared carriers with agent-api as their only writer; marks are one hash field per flag, so a pin and a hide never overwrite each other. | `gate:dataflow` (rail order, rail marks); session index: ungated, reviewed |
| P18 — fail loud | A failed rename, order save, pin, hide, delete or copy shows an error and keeps the old value; the session-list client (`surfaces/sessionsApi.ts`) throws on a backend error instead of returning an empty list. | ungated: reviewed |
| P20 — default-deny | Every route is scoped to the caller's own index; a session that is not yours is `404`; a row you cannot see cannot be pinned or hidden; a failed meeting lookup denies. | deny tests below |
| One state, server-held | Pins, hides and delete live on the server per person, not in one browser's storage. | tests below |

**Security**

- **Who can see and change what.** Only you can list, name, order or read the history of your
  chats. agent-api takes the subject from the gateway's signed identity, never from the request
  body, and a request that names nobody is refused with `401`.
- **What is refused.** A rename of a session not in your index (`404`); a title that is empty or over
  100 characters (`422`); a session id outside the session-name rule, in a rename, an order or a
  history path (`422`); an order entry that is not a row key (`422`); an order with duplicates or more than 5000 entries (`422`); an agent claiming
  a human name (`422`); the agent overwriting a human name (no change); a pin or hide on a chat that is
  not yours or a meeting you cannot read (`404`); a mark request with no flag or an extra field (`422`);
  a delete of a session that is not yours (`404`), of a chat with queued messages (`409`), or by an
  agent acting for you (`403`).
- **Shared meetings.** Your pins and hides on a meeting shared with you are stored under your own
  subject. The owner's list never changes.
- **Delete removes only your own things.** The transcript and pointer are removed only from the
  workspace roots the history route would search for you, by descriptor, so a planted link is never
  followed. Workspace pages are never touched.
- **History.** The server searches only your own workspaces and those your memberships mount. A
  session it does not find there is `404`, whoever else has a thread of that name.
