New-person onboarding reviews the last 90 days of selected connected email and primary calendars,
then follows relevant older threads and reconciles a sourced private graph of people, companies,
projects and meetings. Account-scoped research checkpoints survive turn interruptions; a batch
advances only after graph source receipts or explicit exclusions. Gmail thread reads include older
messages and CC/thread headers; calendar reads retain organizers, attendees and recurrence evidence.
Traversal completion is distinct from the agent's graph synthesis and coverage review. Attachments
and secondary calendars remain outside this first pass; interrupted work resumes on continuation.
