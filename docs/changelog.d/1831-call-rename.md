- **Rename a call.** In the navigator's **Calls** view, point at a call and choose **⋯ → Rename**, or
  press <kbd>F2</kbd> on it. On a meeting's page, click its name in the header. The name is the
  meeting's own title, so the call list, the chat list, the header and the tab all show it. Clear the
  name to go back to the default. Only the call's owner can rename it. See
  [Pages](/minutes/pages#rename-a-call).
- **Calls you deleted no longer appear in Calls**, and a call whose bot never got in is listed at the
  time you sent the bot instead of under "No date". The row's buttons now take the place of its time
  instead of covering it.
