- **Added (API):** `GET /meetings/completion-summary` — how your meetings are ending, counted for you. Each meeting has carried a typed `completion_reason` since v0.12.16, but one at a time: to see that a third of your runs die in the lobby you had to page your whole history and tally the field yourself. The endpoint returns counts per `status` and per `completion_reason` over an optional `since`/`until`/`platform` window, with terminal meetings that recorded no reason named in an explicit `unrecorded` bucket rather than dropped. It publishes **counts only — no rate, ratio or percentage**: the ten reasons are not commensurable (a bot you stopped, a meeting nobody attended and a bot that could not join are all "not completed" and mean opposite things), so a single ratio over them invites the wrong denominator. The reference now documents all ten values and which are the caller's or the host's doing rather than ours.
