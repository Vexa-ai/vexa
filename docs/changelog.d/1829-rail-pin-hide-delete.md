- **The chat list is newest first, with pins, hide and a real delete.** A chat you start now appears
  at the top; before, once you had dragged a row, every new chat landed below all the rows you had
  ever placed. Pin a chat or meeting to keep it on top, and hide one to take it out of the list —
  both are saved for your account and come back on every device. Delete now deletes the chat and its
  conversation on the server (workspace pages stay); a meeting's chat no longer reappears as the
  meeting's own row after you delete it. Chats deleted the old, browser-only way become hidden the
  first time you sign in. The row menu has keyboard shortcuts — `⇧⌘P` pin, `⇧⌘X` hide,
  `⌘⌫` delete (it still asks), `⇧⌘E` new chat; Ctrl instead of ⌘ off a Mac — and `P`, `H`, `D` while
  the menu is open. (#1829) See [Manage your chats](/how-to/chat-controls).
