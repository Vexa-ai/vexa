- **Vexa credits (behind the `usage` switch, off by default).** Each model call is debited in credits at its market cost × `credit_markup` (1.3); a person's credits fill a 5-hour and a weekly window, `GET /api/usage` shows them as percentages, and a full window refuses the next turn with the time it is back unless extra usage is on (`POST /api/usage`; a top-up amount is recorded only, nothing is charged).
- **The usage meter and level picker in the app.** With `usage` on, "42% · 5h · 18% · week" sits beside the account, with a popover of both windows and "+ extra usage". A turn refused for a full window shows when the window is back, "+ extra usage" and "Wait". With `levels` on (hosted plans), a new chat picks a level from 1 to 5 in place of the model picker; after the first message the level is locked and can only be changed by an explicit switch.
