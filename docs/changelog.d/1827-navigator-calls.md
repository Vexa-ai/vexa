- **Calls in the page navigator.** The navigator at the left of the pages panel has a **Files | Calls**
  switch. **Calls** lists the meetings you own and the ones shared with you, newest first and grouped
  by date. Click one to open its transcript. The filter matches call titles, and `>` searches the
  transcripts of your own calls. A call that belongs to a workspace links to that workspace. See
  [Pages](/minutes/pages#find-a-call).
