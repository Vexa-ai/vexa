- **Artifacts are no longer emailed for records that are not meetings.** A new pre-send eligibility gate runs before any meeting artifact goes out and returns one of three outcomes — deliver to every participant, deliver to the meeting's creator only, or produce nothing. It was written after rendering artifacts over a real 22-recording archive found that 2 of them were not meetings at all (played-back video audio; a bot left running in a private room) and both produced clean, sendable artifacts. Uncertainty holds rather than broadcasts, and a record that looks like private domestic conversation is held regardless of every other signal.
