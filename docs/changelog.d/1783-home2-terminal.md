- **The attention home, behind `home2` (off by default) (#1783).** Each workspace on the home is a
  status card: what needs you (up to two, each with its act), what is working there, or "All quiet".
  The composer sticks to the top and takes the colour of the workspace it writes to; a card click
  points it there (never at a workspace you can only read) and opens the workspace page in the right
  panel, with sections for now, needs you, rooms, your chats, routines, calls, cards, files, people,
  activity and metrics. A Now strip at the top lists live calls and what is waiting on you. Pages in
  the right panel can use typed references, badges, columns, callouts, stats, allow-listed actions and
  ```view blocks. Plain tables can be searched, filtered and sorted.
- **Making a workspace from the home (#1783, `home2`).** "+ New workspace" asks for a name, what it is
  for, an optional site and who can see it. The workspace appears as a card with a seeded README; its
  image is proposed (the site's logo, or a mark Vexa designs) and you accept or decline it on the card;
  invitations are drafted in your chat for you to send.
