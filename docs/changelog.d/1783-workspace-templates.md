- **Workspace templates.** Ask *"create a workspace for our meetup from the event template"* and the
  agent asks a few questions and creates a workspace with its pages filled in. Four ship: Event
  organization, Client project, Meeting series / community, Research — each with suggested routines
  that stay off until you ask. Save any workspace as your own template. The company's templates live
  in `_global/workspace-templates/`, are edited by the admin account, and are never overwritten by an
  upgrade. An empty chat now offers **Create a shared workspace**. New tools:
  `workspace_templates_list`, `workspace_create`, `workspace_template_save`. See
  [Workspace templates](/minutes/workspace-templates).
