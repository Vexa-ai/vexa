- **The terminal fits any window, and its secondary text is readable (#1800).** As the window narrows, the chat list folds to a strip and opens as a drawer, then the pages panel becomes a sheet; the conversation keeps its width, and on a phone it is the whole screen. Both side panes resize from the keyboard and remember their width. Metadata no longer breaks mid-word, the composer toolbar never wraps, and tabs that do not fit are listed in an overflow menu. Tertiary text now meets WCAG AA in both themes, and the interface uses Geist. Builders can see every token and control at `/design` when `VEXA_TERMINAL_DESIGN_CATALOGUE=1`. See [Terminal UI design](/governance/ui-design).
