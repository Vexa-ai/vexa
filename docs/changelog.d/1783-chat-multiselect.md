- **Choose several chats at once in the rail.** Cmd/Ctrl-click toggles a chat, Shift-click takes a
  range, Esc clears; dragging any chosen chat moves them all, and a bar offers Move to…, Share… and
  Delete with one confirmation naming the count. On a phone, a long press shows checkboxes. See
  [Manage your chats](/how-to/chat-controls).
