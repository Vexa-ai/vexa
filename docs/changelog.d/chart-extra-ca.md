- Helm: `global.extraCA` adds your root CA for corporate TLS inspection. Name a ConfigMap or Secret holding the PEM, and every Vexa Deployment and Job, and every bot and agent-worker Pod the runtime spawns, trusts it: the PEM is mounted read-only, an init container appends it to the image's system bundle in an emptyDir, and `SSL_CERT_FILE`, `REQUESTS_CA_BUNDLE`, `CURL_CA_BUNDLE`, `GIT_SSL_CAINFO`, `AWS_CA_BUNDLE` and `NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS` point at it. The bot imports it into Chromium's NSS store. Works under OpenShift restricted-v2; unset renders exactly as before.
