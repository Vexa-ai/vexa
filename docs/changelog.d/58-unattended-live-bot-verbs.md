- **A turn with nobody watching can no longer stop or speak through your bot.** A routine's turn, an
  email's turn and a call signal's turn are refused stopping your live bot, changing its settings and
  posting in the meeting chat. A routine you granted `bot.stop` may still stop it. Your own requests
  and the turns you watch in chat are unchanged. See
  [Security → Untrusted triggers](/security-compliance#untrusted-triggers-routines-mail-and-room-calls).
