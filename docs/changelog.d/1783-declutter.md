- **A calmer chat header and routine strip (#1783).** The header is the chat's name (it shortens,
  never covered), the profile chip and, while a meeting the chat started is happening, one chip —
  "● Live · the meeting’s name" — that opens the meeting in the pages panel, where its transcript and Stop bot
  are. The meeting also opens there by itself when it starts. The separate "Stop bot" and
  "Transcript" buttons, the "Chat ›" prefix and the status dot are gone; "Just you" is not shown until
  someone else is in the chat. A routine's thread starts with one line — "Routine · every weekday
  23:00 · next Thu 23:00 · ok" — with the next run as a date; expanded, it says When / Does / Results
  go to, what the routine may do with nobody there ("May send bots: no — allow?", which asks in the
  chat), and a refused run as a sentence. The flow graph and raw schedule are under "Details".
- **A calmer chat list and named turns (#1783).** A row in the chat list is its name and time, with
  at most one icon — call, voice note, shared, or routine — instead of text chips that could hide the
  name. The unfiled section is called **Recent**. In a chat, every turn says who wrote it: "You" or
  "Vexa", the same way other people's messages show their names.
