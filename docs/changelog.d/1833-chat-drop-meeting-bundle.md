- **Import a call by dropping its bundle into a chat (#1833).** Drag a meeting bundle (`*.meeting-bundle.zip`,
  exported from any Vexa deployment) onto any chat, paste it, or attach it, and the chat imports it
  as a call instead of attaching it: a card shows the progress, then the call's title as a link to its
  transcript, or that you already imported it, or the deployment's reason for refusing it. Other files
  dropped with it attach as usual. See [Move a meeting between deployments](/how-to/move-a-meeting#import).
