- **Cards are obligations with one next actor.** A commitment, approval or question now names the
  one person whose move it is and follows a fixed route. You accept a card before it is yours, a
  delivery waits for the person who asked to confirm it, and every decision is recorded with the card
  you saw. Decisions, and in-chat answers to an agent's question, are made only from the signed-in
  app — never with an API key or by an agent. See
  [Security → Decisions on cards](/security-compliance#decisions-on-cards-who-moves-an-obligation).
