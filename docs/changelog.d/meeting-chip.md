- **Meetings in replies and pages show as a meeting chip.** A meeting a reply or page names now shows
  as a chip beside people and companies, with the meeting's title and date (for example
  *Campbell · Fri 9 Oct*). It opens the meeting. This covers a `/?meeting=` link and a link to the
  meeting's notes page; the notes filename is no longer shown. A meeting you cannot open shows as
  plain text, with no title. The agent now names meetings by their link. See
  [Links in replies](/minutes/chats#links-in-replies).
