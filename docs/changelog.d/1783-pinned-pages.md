- **Pinned pages are yours, in every chat.** Pinning a page keeps it as a tab in every chat, new and
  existing, in your order; unpinning removes it everywhere. The list is kept for your account by
  agent-api (`GET`/`PUT /api/user/pinned-pages`). While the right-hand panel is closed, pinned pages
  show as small chips in the chat header that open the panel on that page. The agent is told your
  pinned pages (title and address, never their content), so "update my unanswered page" finds it.
