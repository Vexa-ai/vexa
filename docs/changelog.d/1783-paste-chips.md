- **Long pastes collapse.** Past 15 lines or 1,500 characters, a paste becomes a chip above the
  composer ("Pasted text · 214 lines" and its first line): × removes it, a click opens it back into
  the box to edit. The agent still receives the whole text; the sent bubble shows your own words and
  the collapsed chip, which opens on click. Long messages already in a chat's history collapse the
  same way after a reload.
