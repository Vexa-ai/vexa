- **Leaving a workspace takes you out of its closed rooms.** A person removed from a workspace, or one
  who left it, could still read and post in its closed rooms and start the room's agent there. Now a
  closed room's membership counts only while the person is in the room's workspace, and their room rows
  are dropped when the roster changes. If they rejoin the workspace, a member of the closed room adds
  them again.
