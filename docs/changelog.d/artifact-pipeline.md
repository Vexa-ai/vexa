- **A completed meeting now becomes per-participant artifacts by itself.** A new artifact-pipeline service joins the pieces that already existed and were not connected: it fetches the record over the public API, runs the pre-send eligibility gate, renders one context delta per participant — *what changed in your context because this meeting happened* — hands each to the artifact postman, and appends an audit line naming the verdict, the recipients and what happened to each of them. The gate runs **before** rendering, so a record that is not a meeting produces no artifact at all rather than one that is filtered later. Delivery is idempotent per (meeting, recipient), and the artifact now has a schema with a canonical markdown emitter, replacing a prose contract that let two renderers emit incompatible headers and point a record link at the wrong meeting. The deterministic renderer quotes only sentences the record can be made to prove; the model-driven renderer is declared and raises until a workspace model route exists.
