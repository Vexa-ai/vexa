- **Start a meeting offers who or where.** Call a person, start in a room, or schedule for later (planned
  in a room; nothing reaches a calendar until you add it). A Vexa call is the default; Meet, Teams and
  Zoom are under Other options. See [Rooms](/minutes/rooms).
- **No duplicate calls.** Starting a call where one of yours is live asks "… is still running. Rejoin
  it?" (Rejoin / Start a new one), including when you call a person you are already in a call with.
- **`@` in private chats.** The picker offers the people you share a workspace with; a pick tags them
  or calls them in your room of two. The chat stays private.
- **Calls are named after who or where** ("Call with Maya", "#pilot call") and renamed by topic once
  there is one; the row says "renamed".
- **Live → Recent.** Live rows show who is in now and the elapsed time; a call ends when the last person
  leaves and moves to Recent with "ended when everyone left · duration".
