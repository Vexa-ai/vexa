- **Prep in a planned meeting, and Prepare from Home (#1783; features `meeting_prep` and
  `home_prepare`, off by default, on by name).** A planned meeting's panel shows a Prep section above
  its notes: your goal (the `goals:` line on the meeting's page; private on your desk copy, marked as
  shared when the page lives in a shared workspace), the people invited as you know them and where
  from, the open cards from the previous occurrence, the agenda and the material written on the page,
  and three readiness ticks worked out when it is read. Nothing new is stored: every line is read from
  the meeting, your people, your cards and the meeting's page, and setting the goal writes that page.
  Home's Now strip shows the next planned meeting with Prepare. One click opens the meeting with its
  Prep on the right, opens the chat about the meeting in the centre (or starts one in the meeting's
  workspace), and focuses the composer with the meeting's full reference as a badge.
