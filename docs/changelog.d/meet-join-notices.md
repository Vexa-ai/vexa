- **Google Meet calls with Gemini notes or another notetaker.** Meet shows every joiner a notice,
  "Gemini is taking notes" or "Your call audio and video will be shared with …", with Leave and Join
  now. The bot used to wait at that notice until its lobby budget ran out and fail with "no meeting
  indicators found", or sit in the call hearing nothing. It now clicks "Join now" inside these
  notices only, never "Leave". A prompt without "Join now" still waits for a person. Thanks to
  @jbschooley (#1251) and @manoloalvarez (#1727).
