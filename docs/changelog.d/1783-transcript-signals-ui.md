- **What Vexa listens for in a meeting is on the meeting page (#1783).** When transcript signals are on
  (`transcript_signals`), the meeting page shows one line, "Listening for: …", with the labels that
  wake Vexa; clicking it opens a plain editor, and saving writes the change into the page, where the
  meeting's agent reads it. "To Vexa" is always listened for. Transcript lines carry a small chip when
  they were a decision, an action, a blocker or said to Vexa, and their most significant words read
  heavier; without signals the transcript is unchanged. A reply Vexa gives because the meeting woke it
  is marked "From the meeting" in the meeting's chat.
