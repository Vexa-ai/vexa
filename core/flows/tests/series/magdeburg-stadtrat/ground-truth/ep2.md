# Episode 2 — 2026-02-26 · 030.(VIII) Sitzung · was tatsächlich passiert ist

> **DERIVED.** Distilled by us from the published Niederschrift
> ([PDF](https://ratsinfo.magdeburg.de/getfile.asp?id=738820&type=do), 48 pp). Not the minutes.
> Where the two disagree, the PDF wins.
>
> **Scope:** first 35 min of video ≈ first ~25 min of session, i.e. TOP 1–4 and the opening of
> TOP 5. The session ran 14:00–20:30.

**The room.** Same chair (Schwenke), same executive (Borris). 56 seats; **45 present at the
gavel, 51 at peak, 4 excused** — a fuller house than episode 1. Note the session number jumps
028 → 030: the January sitting was continued on 26.01 as 029.(VIII), which is why episode 1's
minutes are confirmed here as "028./029.".

**What this episode tests is continuity.** Nothing structurally new happens. The skeleton is the
same — Eröffnung, Tagesordnung, Niederschrift, Bekanntgabe, Beschlussfassung — and the value of
the episode in the series is whether a desk built on episode 1 recognises the room, the roles and
the factions without being told again, and carries the open threads rather than re-deriving them.

**TOP 1 · Eröffnung.** Quorum established. On the AfD's motion the council confirms three
committee substitutions (Beschluss-Nr. **1017-030(VIII)26**): Kevin Michalzik replaces Christian
Mertens on the Vergabeausschuss; Steffen Kraus replaces Hagen Kohl on the committee for municipal
legal and citizen affairs; Kevin Michalzik replaces Steffen Kraus on the Betriebsausschuss
Konservatorium. Schwenke then announces the death of former Stadtrat **Alfred Westphal** on
06.01.2026 and honours his long voluntary service; the council holds **a minute's silence**.

**TOP 2 · Bestätigung der Tagesordnung.** One addition (DS0035/26, construction contracts for the
third phase of the Stadthalle surroundings, taken into the **non-public** part as TOP 12.3, by
two-thirds). Deferrals: AfD on A0062/25 and A0047/26; SPD/Tierschutzallianz/Volt on A0027/25;
Gartenpartei on A0226/25 (carried by majority, not unanimously). CDU/FDP and
SPD/Tierschutzallianz/Volt jointly send DS0603/25 back to the BSS and Juhi committees,
unanimously.

**The thread that opens here.** On CDU/FDP's motion the council agrees **by majority** to debate
TOP 6.9 — **A0224/25, "Strategie für die Entwicklung des Intel-Geländes im High-Tech Park
Magdeburg"** — in the **non-public** part. A single procedural line, and it is the beginning of
the fight that consumes episode 3's opening. GRÜNE/future! have TOPs 6.25 (A0020/26) and 6.44
(A0030/26) taken together. The amended agenda passes unanimously.

**TOP 3.** Minutes of the 028./029.(VIII) sessions of 22./26.01.2026 confirmed, **42 yes, 0 no,
0 abstentions** — episode 1's record, formally closed in episode 2.

**TOP 4.** Resolutions taken in committees and in non-public session: noted.

**TOP 5 · Beschlussfassung** begins with 5.1, amending the partnership agreement of Magdeburger
Hafen GmbH. Most of TOP 5 is beyond the fixture's window.

**Still running, not resolved:** the **Haushaltssperre** and the 11 M€ consolidation concept from
episode 1; the **Landesverwaltungsamt** is referenced a dozen times across this Niederschrift, far
more than in episode 1 — a scaffold that noticed the supervisory authority becoming the dominant
external actor would be reading the series correctly.
