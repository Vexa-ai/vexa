# Episode 1 — 2026-01-22 · 028.(VIII) Sitzung · was tatsächlich passiert ist

> **DERIVED.** Distilled by us from the published Niederschrift
> ([PDF](https://ratsinfo.magdeburg.de/getfile.asp?id=736261&type=do), 53 pp). Not the minutes.
> Where the two disagree, the PDF wins.
>
> **Scope:** the fixture holds the first 35 min of video ≈ the first ~25 min of session, i.e.
> roughly TOP 1–6. The session ran 14:00–21:00. Everything after TOP 6 is in the PDF and out of
> the fixture's reach — a scaffold is not wrong for missing it.

**The room.** Chair: Wigbert Schwenke, Vorsitzender des Stadtrates. Executive:
Oberbürgermeisterin Simone Borris. 56 seats; **42 present at the gavel, 51 at peak, 6 excused**.
The Niederschrift names every one of them.

**TOP 1 · Eröffnung.** Schwenke opens the 028th session of the VIII electoral period, confirms
the invitation was proper and the council quorate. On the Jugendhilfeausschuss's motion the
council appoints Ulrike Stroka as an advisory member from the Stadtelternvertretung, with Anika
Graniowski as her deputy (Beschluss-Nr. **957-028(VIII)26**).

**TOP 2 · Verabschiedung.** Borris and then Schwenke thank the outgoing Ortsbürgermeister of
Randau/Calenberge, **Günther Kräuter**, for his long service; Kräuter thanks the council and the
administration in return. A ceremonial item — and a good test of whether a scaffold can tell a
farewell from a decision.

**TOP 3 · Bestätigung der Tagesordnung.** The agenda fight. One addition (DS0593/25, quay
rebuild MK 2a in the Industriehafen, added as TOP 14.4 by two-thirds). The Oberbürgermeisterin
defers DS0577/25, DS0356/25, DS0357/25; SPD/Tierschutzallianz/Volt withdraws A0159/25; AfD
defers A0180/25 and withdraws A0102/25/1; CDU/FDP asks for DS0585/25 to go back to the BSS and
Juhi committees and for A0167/25 to be voted point by point. The amended agenda passes
unanimously.

**TOP 4.** Minutes of the 026./027.(VIII) session of 04./08.12.2025 confirmed, **41 yes, 0 no,
1 abstention**.

**TOP 5 · and the item that actually matters.** After the formal notice, the Oberbürgermeisterin
delivers an **Information zur Haushaltssituation**. She had intended to brief the council in the
non-public part on the full conditions the **Landesverwaltungsamt** attached to the approved
budget, but a press release from that authority pre-empted her, which she regrets. The
conditions: investments cut by roughly **5.3 million €**, commitment authorisations reduced by
**7.14 million €**, an **immediate spending freeze (Haushaltssperre)**, with a target of saving
**11 million €** in total. A binding **Haushaltskonsolidierungskonzept** must be worked out with
the council by **30 November 2026**. She calls further personnel cuts critical — there are already
nearly **500 unfilled posts** and the Flexliste has been used already — and says she will resist
anything that ends in closing facilities. Objecting to the conditions is judged difficult; having
an approved budget at all beats provisional budget management. A Drucksache on joining the budget
is planned for February.

**TOP 6 · Widerspruch der Oberbürgermeisterin.** The executive formally objects to the council's
own resolution of 04.12.2025 on unifying event-registration formalities (Antrag **A0200/25**,
Fraktion Gartenpartei). Zander (Gartenpartei) asks for the objection and the motion to be debated
together. Müller (Die Linke) wanted more explanation and criticises that the objection was never
flagged when the motion passed. Borris separates two things: this is not about the substance —
the administration must implement the citizens' will — but about her **Organisationshoheit**,
which she says the motion intrudes on unlawfully, and she warns that under the spending freeze
she may have to object more often. Linke (GRÜNE/future!) calls the objection "zu krass" and wants
prior agreement on how motions are drafted. Kumpf (AfD) wants it back in committee. The council
**joins the objection: 19 yes, 16 no, 10 abstentions** (Beschluss-Nr. **958-028(VIII)26**), which
implicitly rejects A0200/25.
