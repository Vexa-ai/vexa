# Episode 3 — 2026-03-26 · 032.(VIII) Sitzung · was tatsächlich passiert ist

> **DERIVED.** Distilled by us from the published Niederschrift
> ([PDF](https://ratsinfo.magdeburg.de/getfile.asp?id=740939&type=do), 47 pp). Not the minutes.
> Where the two disagree, the PDF wins.
>
> **Scope:** first 35 min of video ≈ first ~25 min of session — TOP 1 and the whole of TOP 2,
> which is where this episode's substance is. The session ran 14:00–20:40.

**The room.** Schwenke chairs, Borris for the executive. 56 seats; **47 present at the gavel, 52
at peak, 5 excused** — the fullest house of the three. On the Jugendhilfeausschuss's motion, Herr
Saaied is appointed deputy to advisory member Frau Weidner (Beschluss-Nr. **1066-032(VIII)26**).

**The episode opens on a warning about recusal.** Before anything else Schwenke raises
**Mitwirkungsverbot** — conflict-of-interest recusal under § 33 KVG LSA — "aus aktuellem Anlass":
a town north of Magdeburg had a resolution struck down by the Landesverwaltungsamt because a
councillor took part who should not have. He asks every member to check their own position:
professional grounds, personal grounds, and family relations all count. This is not procedure for
its own sake — it pays off within the hour, when Stadtrat Schache declares his own recusal and
steps out of a vote.

He also notes that because a **Grundsatzaussprache** is scheduled (TOP 5, the administration's
strategy paper **"Magdeburg2040"**), questions to the administration will be answered in writing
under § 9 of the rules of procedure, and reminds the room of the speaking times under § 12.

**TOP 2 · the agenda fight, and it is the meeting.** Two disputes:

*First, DS0066/26.* The Landesverwaltungsamt has ruled it must be handled in the **public** part.
Guderjahn (Gartenpartei) objects that it was not in the invitation, so adding it needs a
two-thirds majority; Schwenke says it was on the non-public agenda. Kumpf (AfD) backs Guderjahn,
asks for a legal check, and criticises the late forwarding of the authority's letter — Schwenke
answers the letter arrived two days earlier. Zander (Gartenpartei) reinforces; Guderjahn moves to
defer the item. Rohne (CDU/FDP) argues a corrected second invitation was uploaded on the day of
summons, so the deadline holds. **Dr. Ignatuschtschenko (BLin BOB)** explains a technical fault
left the Drucksache out of the invitation, which was re-uploaded correctly about an hour later.

*Second, the Intel site — and a contested vote.* The council adds **A0226/25** (sale of the city's
stake in Magdeburger Weihnachtsmarkt mbH) by two-thirds as TOP 7.17. Then the interfractional
motion **A0067/26 — "Rückkauf des Intel-Grundstückes am Eulenberg / Sicherung kommunaler
Einflussmöglichkeiten"**. Borris opposes putting it on the agenda at all: she wants a **special
session** to go into it properly. Rohne (CDU/FDP) wants no more time lost. Schwenke declares the
two-thirds reached; **Borris challenges the count** — with 49 members present it was not.
Schwenke rules a re-vote. Dr. Grube (SPD/Tierschutzallianz/Volt) doubts a re-vote is lawful once a
result exists; Kumpf (AfD) sides with the chair; Schwenke holds that a challenged result may be
re-taken. **Schache (CDU/FDP) declares his Mitwirkungsverbot under § 33 KVG LSA and does not
vote.** With 50 present the council adds the motion: **37 yes, 12 no, 0 abstentions**.

Deferrals follow (CDU/FDP on DS0287/25, TOP 6.20, unanimously) — routine after the above.

**What the series makes visible and one meeting cannot.** The Intel item was pushed into the
non-public part in episode 2 and forced back into the open in episode 3 by the supervisory
authority and an interfractional majority, over the executive's objection. The recusal warning
that opens the session is answered by a recusal inside it. And the actor with the most influence
over all three sessions — the **Landesverwaltungsamt** — has never been in the room.
