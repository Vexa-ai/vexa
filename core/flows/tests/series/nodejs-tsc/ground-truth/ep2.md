A well-attended, short-agenda meeting — **nine voting members**, roughly half of them new to the
series after episode 1's six. The published agenda is unusually thin: four items across four
repos, no Strategic Initiatives section at all.

**What episode 2 tests is continuity, not content.** Almost nothing here is new. The value of
this episode in the series is that a scaffold built on episode 1 should now (a) recognise most of
the room without being told again, (b) notice that the attendee set has grown and changed, and
(c) carry the open threads forward rather than re-deriving them from scratch.

**Announced:** the security releases promised in episode 1 are **out** — an episode-1 commitment
closing in episode 2, which is exactly the kind of link a single-meeting note cannot make.

**Fallout from that release:** Docker images are still missing for the security release
([nodejs/docker-node#2424](https://github.com/nodejs/docker-node/issues/2424)) — a *new* problem
that only exists because of the episode-1 item.

**Threads carried from episode 1:**
- Ownership of Rust crates ([nodejs/admin#1045](https://github.com/nodejs/admin/issues/1045)) —
  still open, third appearance across the series.
- Meeting schedule ([nodejs/TSC#1832](https://github.com/nodejs/TSC/issues/1832)) — the TSC's own
  cadence under revision.

**New this episode:** doc-deprecate `module.register()`
([nodejs/node#62395](https://github.com/nodejs/node/pull/62395)).

**Notably absent:** the AI-contributions vote is *not* on this agenda. It was deliberately parked
in episode 1 for the 1st of April. A scaffold that reports the thread as dropped has misread it;
a scaffold that reports it as scheduled has read the series correctly.

**The recurring skeleton holds** — Announcements, Reminders, CPC and Board Meeting Updates
(empty this week), per-repo sections, Upcoming Meetings. The empty CPC section is itself a
signal: the structure is a template the organizer fills, not a summary of what happened.
